# Evidence from the corpus

The numbers behind this skill. They come from a research corpus of Reddit mental-health threads
(217 posts, 14 subreddits, about 8,500 sentences) coded sentence by sentence by a team of
annotators with a 23-label CBT codebook and adjudicated to a final label set. Only the sentences
written by responders (not the original poster) are summarized here, and only the "dev" half of
the posts was used to write the skill; the other half is held out to test it.

## What responders' sentences do (rate per 100 responder sentences)

| Move | per 100 sentences | share of replies containing it |
|---|---|---|
| Self-disclosure ("this happened to me") | 32 | very common |
| Recommendation | 28 | 84% of posts get at least one |
| Restructuring / reframing | 11 | |
| Psychoeducation | 5 | |
| Validation | 5 (but see placement below) | 61% of posts |
| A question back to the poster (Focal Point: Request) | 4 | |
| Cognitive conceptualization ("because...") | 3 | |
| Grounding technique | 2 | |
| Encouraging self-compassion | 2 | |
| Clinical referral | 2 | 23% of posts |
| Social support | 2 | |
| Summarizing | 1 | |
| Bad Advice | 0.6 | |
| Incorrect Information | 0.4 | |
| Non-expert diagnosis | 0.1 | |

Self-disclosure is the biggest thing human peers do that this skill cannot honestly imitate.
Compensate with specificity, normalization, and warmth, not with invented stories.

## Where each move sits inside a reply (replies with three or more sentences)

Rate per 100 sentences at that position:

| Move | first sentence | middle | last sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Validation | 20 | 2 | 2 |
| Self-disclosure | 24 | 29 | 20 |
| Recommendation | 17 | 33 | 27 |
| Restructuring | 7 | 13 | 8 |
| Question back to the poster | 6 | 2 | 6 |
| Psychoeducation | 4 | 8 | 2 |
| Clinical referral | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Encouraging self-compassion | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Grounding technique | 0 | 3 | 1 |

Reading: validation is an opening move; suggestions and reframes are the body; endings are a
final suggestion, encouragement, or a question. This is the shape the skill asks for.

## How long good replies are

First replies to a post: median 5 sentences and 79 words; the middle half runs 3 to 8 sentences
and 38 to 116 words; one in ten runs past 15 sentences or 285 words (usually answering a long,
multi-part post). All replies including short follow-ups: median 4 sentences, 48 words.

What recommendations travelled with, when they were not alone: the responder's own experience
(most common), a reframe, a grounding script, a referral, social support, a self-compassion
nudge, a brief explanation of why it works.

## Focal points: what posters come for

At the title level, 72% of posts are a request for advice, help, information or opinion, 14%
are a statement or update, and 11% are a complaint or vent. Answering the request is the job.

## Codebook rules that show up in the numbers

- Every clinical referral in the corpus was also a recommendation (87 of 87): referrals were
  concrete, not "see someone".
- Encouraging self-compassion was a recommendation four times out of five.
- Validation was tagged once per reply (first instance), which is also how it reads best.
